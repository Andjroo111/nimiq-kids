Roadmap

What comes next

Live now

A chore app built on Nimiq

  • Any browser, or a mini app inside Nimiq Pay
  • A mini wallet built into the page: no app store, no extension, no seed phrase
  • Any device is theirs once they connect it
  • Five languages
  • Earn for chores, routines and activities. Spend it in the Treasure Box. The money circles back to you
  • Stake it and watch it earn while it sits

They are learning to be their own bank with the training wheels still on: they run it, they stake it, they spend it, and none of it leaves their wallet unless you approve.

August

  1. One month

    Testing it with my own kids

    My kids run their real chore routines on it for a full month, on our own tablets. The feedback button is live in both apps, in the corner menu next to the language picker.

  2. Community funding

    A Rive animator to gamify the app

    A Nimiq Community Funding proposal, $5,000 to $10,000, for a Rive animator. Quotes from two or three I already know in the Rive community. The bar is Duolingo: a character that reacts when a chore is approved, a streak that builds, coins that fly into the balance, a celebration when a whole routine lands.

  3. Building now

    Recurring allowance and bonuses

    An allowance the parent sets and controls, that repeats, plus bonuses.

September

  1. Distribution

    A grassroots push to get families on it

    Most crypto asks people to invest first. This solves a problem first. I have young kids, so I am around parents constantly: I hand them a cashlink and ask them to try it at home. Real families first, then an App Store listing, then marketing once there is something to point at.

Future goals

  1. Cosmetics

    Creator drops, the way Fortnite does it

    New sticker packs, backgrounds and characters, released in seasons. Outside creators make them too: a creator submits a pack, I approve it into the app, then each parent decides whether their own kid can buy it. Two gates before anything reaches a kid.

  2. Integrations

    Research phase

    Paying kids for learning too

    Connecting to apps that teach reading, math, music and coding. Ello, Khan Academy Kids, Yousician. Nothing that is only a game. HTLCs would release the reward once the work is confirmed.

Always

No ads, no tracking, nothing predatory

The parent funds everything and approves every chore before it pays. It stays open source.
