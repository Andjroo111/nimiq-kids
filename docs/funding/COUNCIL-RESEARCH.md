**Compiled 2026-08-03** · sources: forum.nimiq.community (all 13 proposals read), nimiq.com/blog

## 1. Board vs Council: which way it goes

**Board came first. Council is the current body.**

| When | Body | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 to 2021 | **Community Funding Board (CFB)** | "Community Project Funding" meetings 1 through 15, blogged on nimiq.com and Medium. Reps elected Q4 2020. |
| Jul 2022 | **CFB re-activated** | 6 members: 3 community-elected plus 3 Team Nimiq. Community reps re-elected every 6 months. **Annual budget up to $50k USD.** Then went quiet again. |
| Jan 23 2026 | Legal & Ethical Guideline Framework posted (by Micha) | The rulebook the current body runs on. |
| Feb 18 to Mar 6 2026 | **Nimiq Community Council election** | 13 nominees, 5 elected. |
| Mar 23 and 24 2026 | Forum category **Proposals & Bounties** (id 51) opens; proposal guidelines posted | This is the live front door. |

So: **CFB (old) became the Nimiq Community Council (current, since March 2026).**
Gotcha: the forum still badges council members with the group tag **`CommunityBoard`**. That is a
leftover label, not a second body.

**Where to submit:** https://forum.nimiq.community/c/proposals-and-bounties/51 or email
`Proposals@Nimiq.com`. Public thread, public decision, public rationale (required by §8 of the
framework).

**Who decides** (identified from the CommunityBoard badge across all threads):
`paulghz` (writes almost every verdict), `Faberto` (Tobi, ex-CFB), `pls_enter`, `wille-io`, `2KidZ`.
`Richy` is the forum admin and moderator, not a voter.

## 3. The pattern: what actually gets you funded

Derived from the verdicts above, not from the guidelines page.

**Disqualifiers (any one of these kills it):**
1. **Bundling.** Two initiatives in one proposal gets rejected, every time. ($11k Nigeria, $72k shadcn)
2. **Asking too much.** Nothing above $4,500 has ever been approved. $6k, $8k, $11k, $30k, $72k and $200k were all rejected.
3. **Vapourware.** The thing must already exist and be usable today. (Jumpa)
4. **Generic.** If the proposal would read the same with another chain's name pasted in, it dies. (ActionFi)
5. **Not on Nimiq.** Wallet-connection-only is explicitly not enough. (PlayMate)
6. **Duplicating what Nimiq already ships.** (Adoption Network vs Nimiq Pay and CPL)
7. **Closed source**, or tied to a commercial operation the Council would effectively be subsidising. (NimiqHub)
8. **AI tool subscriptions as a line item.** They will not fund Cursor or Claude seats by name. They *will* fund "development" and let you spend it however you like. (Nimiq Space)
9. **Gambling or wagering.** Hard prohibition, §4 of the framework.
10. **Vague deliverables.** "Tutorials", "community engagement", "content" without specifics. (Creator Lab, twice)

**What the funded ones did:**
- One thing, described precisely, already half-built.
- Money mostly going to **output**, not to the applicant's salary. (Creator Lab was praised for putting 80% into participant rewards; Nimiq 2048 for being "lean")
- Milestones with **dollar amounts attached to each**, last tranche gated on final delivery.
- Public repo plus a commitment to post progress **in the forum thread**. They chase you for updates: paulghz pinged the Nigeria project three times about missing videos.
- Brandbook compliance offered up front for anything visual. The Council asked for this unprompted on the video project (correct colours, logo, typography).
- Numbers you can be held to (wallet activations, transactions, users), on a public dashboard.

## 4. ⚠️ The blocker: Mini Apps Competition participants cannot be funded

15 July 2026, `pls_enter`, verbatim, on XcrowHub's $1,000 request:

> "The Nimiq Community Council does not provide separate funding to Mini Apps while they are
> actively participating in the Nimiq Mini Apps Competition. […] all Mini Apps must compete under
> the same conditions. Providing additional Community Council funding to an active participant
> would create an unfair advantage over the other projects.
>
> For this reason, marketing campaigns, referral rewards, user acquisition, server costs,
> promotional materials, and other expenses during the competition must be financed by the founder
> or through other funding sources. […]
>
> **Once all three competition cycles have concluded**, a project that did not receive a
> competition prize may submit a new funding proposal to the Nimiq Community Council."

**What this means for nimiq.kids:**
- Cycle III ends **11 October 2026**. That is the earliest unambiguously clean date.
- The nimiq.kids Cycle I submission (PRs #88 and #93) is **still unmerged**, so it is arguable that
  it is not an "active participant" at all. That is an argument, not a fact, and it is the same five
  people who would rule on it.
- The exclusion list they wrote covers marketing, ops and servers. **Art and animation production is
  not on that list.** This is genuinely open, and worth asking rather than assuming.

**Recommended play:** post a short eligibility question in the thread *before* the full proposal.
A public rejection sits permanently in front of the same five people who judge the competition.
A question costs nothing. Draft is in the proposal doc, §0.

## 5. Budget reality check vs the ROADMAP

`docs/ROADMAP.md` currently says the Rive ask is "in the 5 to 10 thousand dollar range."

**That is above the ceiling.** Every request over $4,500 has been rejected by this Council, and the
one that came closest ($6k PlayMate) was refused with a budget note attached. $11k came back as
$2,900. The Council has twice written that its available funds are limited.

**Recommend asking $4,500**, exactly matching the largest sum they have approved, structured in
three tranches, with a Phase-2 option flagged but *not* requested (the Nimiq Space pattern: prove it,
then extension is "on the table"). ROADMAP should be corrected to match.
